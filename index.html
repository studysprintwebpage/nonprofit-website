What's Inside

Everything a care box contains

Each box is packed to cover the health needs we see most often, paired with one clear guide so supplies are actually used with confidence.

🩹
Adhesive Bandages
Assorted sizes for minor cuts, scrapes, and blisters.
🧻
Sterile Gauze Pads
For cleaning wounds and applying pressure to larger abrasions.
🧴
Alcohol Swabs
Single-use antiseptic wipes to disinfect wounds and surfaces.
💊
OTC Medications
Tylenol and Advil for pain, fever, and inflammation relief.
🌡️
Digital Thermometer
A quick, reliable way to check for fever at home, new to every box.
🪥
Toothbrush & Toothpaste
Everything needed for everyday oral care.
📖
Health & First Aid Guide
One easy-to-read booklet covering safe OTC dosing and step-by-step first aid, so nothing is left to guesswork.
How It Works

From assembly to your hands

Care Compass works alongside partner organizations to get boxes into the hands of people who need them most.

01
We source & assemble
Kind donors provide funding, and Care Compass leaders coordinate materials and outreach. Volunteers then pack first aid supplies and OTC medications into every care box by hand.
02
We partner with orgs
Boxes are distributed through trusted community organizations already serving people in need.
03
Boxes reach people
Recipients receive their box with all supplies plus our Health & First Aid Guide — free of charge.
04
Knowledge transfers
The included guide empowers recipients to use supplies safely and understand basic medical care.
"

Access to clear health information shouldn't be a barrier.

Care Compass works to make medication safety simple and accessible for families in our community.

Our Mission

Why we do this

We started Care Compass because we saw a gap: many people lacked not just basic medical supplies, but the knowledge to use them safely. Our boxes solve both problems.

  • ✓Access without barriers. Every box is completely free to the recipient — always.
  • ✓Education alongside resources. Supplies are only half the solution. Our guide builds real health confidence.
  • ✓Community-powered distribution. We work through existing organizations to reach people where they already are.
  • ✓Dignity at the core. Care is delivered without judgment, applications, or conditions.

Privacy Policy

Care Compass App Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Care Compass ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, and protected when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account or use Care Compass, we may collect personal details you provide directly to us, including your name, email address, phone number, and user profile role.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to identify your account, connect you with local community support, allow communication regarding volunteer initiatives, and save your app preferences.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your account information is securely stored using Google Cloud Firestore and encrypted device storage. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third-party advertisers or data brokers for tracking purposes.

4. Account Deletion and Data Control

You can view, edit, or permanently delete your account profile and associated data at any time directly within the Account Settings section of the Care Compass app.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team through our primary website or app support channel.

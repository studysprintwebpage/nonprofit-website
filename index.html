Inside the box

What every care box contains

The right resources are only useful when they are easy to understand. Each box contains the essentials alongside a clear health and first-aid guide.

Adhesive bandagesAssorted sizes for minor cuts, scrapes, and blisters.
Sterile gauze padsFor cleaning wounds and applying pressure to abrasions.
Alcohol swabsSingle-use antiseptic wipes for wounds and surfaces.
OTC medicationsTylenol and Advil for pain, fever, and inflammation relief.
Digital thermometerA quick, reliable way to check for fever at home.
Toothbrush & toothpasteEveryday oral-care essentials.
Health & first aid guidePlain-language medication and first-aid guidance.

Our mission

Why we do this

Care Compass began with a simple observation: too many people lack both basic medical supplies and the information to use them safely. Our care boxes answer both needs at once.

  • Access without barriers. Every box is completely free to the recipient.
  • Education alongside resources. Supplies are paired with practical guidance.
  • Community-powered distribution. We work through organizations people already trust.
  • Dignity at the core. Care is delivered without judgment, applications, or conditions.

Privacy policy

Care Compass App Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Information we collect

When you create an account or use Care Compass, we may collect personal details you provide directly, including your name, email address, phone number, and user profile role.

2. How we use your information

We use your information strictly to identify your account, connect you with local community support, communicate about volunteer initiatives, and save your app preferences.

3. Data storage and security

Your account information is securely stored using Google Cloud Firestore and encrypted device storage. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Account deletion and data control

You can view, edit, or permanently delete your account profile and associated data at any time directly within the Account Settings section of the Care Compass app.

5. Contact us

For privacy questions, contact our support team through our primary website or app support channel.
